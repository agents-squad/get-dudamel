**Version:** 0.4 (draft) · **Last updated:** 2026-11-17

This Privacy Notice explains what personal data **Dudamel LLC** ("**Dudamel**", "**we**") receives
when you use the Dudamel software (the "**Software**"), why, and what you can do about it.
Capitalised terms have the meaning given in the Dudamel End User License Agreement (`EULA.md`).

## 1. What this notice covers, and what it does not

You host the Software on your own infrastructure. **Dudamel does not host your instance and does
not receive the data you put into it** — your workflows, agents, conversations, files, knowledge
or the data of the Third-Party Services you connect. For that data you, not Dudamel, decide what
is processed and how; this notice does not cover it.

This notice covers only the limited data Dudamel itself receives, as an independent controller
(EULA §5.4):

1. the License Key registration and renewal data sent by the Software (section 2); and
2. what you choose to send us yourself, for example by email (section 3).

## 2. License Key registration and renewal

While automatic renewal is enabled (it is unless you disable it, EULA §4.1), the Software contacts
Dudamel's license server:

| When | What the Software sends |
| --- | --- |
| **Registration** — once, after an administrator accepts the EULA, if no License Key is installed | The organization name entered during setup, the **email address of the administrator who accepted the EULA**, and a randomly generated instance identifier |
| **Renewal** — at startup and about every 12 hours | The current License Key (which states the plan, the key's identifier and the customer name) and the instance identifier |

With each request the server also receives its technical data, such as the IP address and the
time of the request.

**Nothing else.** The Software sends no telemetry, usage statistics, error reports or Customer
Data to Dudamel. Before an administrator accepts the EULA, the Software sends nothing at all. If
you disable automatic renewal, no registration or renewal takes place.

**Why we use it:** to issue, renew and verify License Keys, to tell the plan of each instance
apart, to prevent abuse of the free plan, and to contact the administrator about the license, the
terms or security issues that affect the Software.

**Legal basis** (where the GDPR or a similar law applies): the performance of the EULA, which
needs a License Key for each instance (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR), and our legitimate interest in
protecting the Software against abuse and in keeping our records (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

## 3. Data you send us yourself

If you write to us — support, a security or abuse report, an arbitration opt-out, a legal
notice — we receive your email address, your name if you include it, and the content of your
message. We use it only to answer you and to handle the matter you raised, and keep it as long as
needed for that and for the legal records it may form part of.

## 4. Who receives the data

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it for advertising. We share it only:

- with the providers that host or operate our license server and email for us, under a contract
  that lets them use it only on our behalf;
- with authorities when the law requires it, or to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

Those providers may be located outside your country, including in the United States. Where the
law requires it, we rely on appropriate safeguards for such transfers, such as the European
Commission's standard contractual clauses.

## 5. How long we keep it

- **Registration and renewal data:** while the instance holds a License Key and for **twelve (12)
  months** after its last renewal, then it is deleted, except what we must keep longer to comply
  with the law or to resolve a dispute.
- **Request technical data (IP address, time):** in our server logs for up to **ninety (90)
  days**.
- **Your messages:** as long as needed to handle them, as described in section 3.

## 6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access the personal data we hold about you,
to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability,
and to withdraw any consent you gave. To exercise them, write to **dudamel.support@gmail.com**. We
will answer within the period the law sets (one month under the GDPR).

Deleting the administrator's email may stop us from renewing the instance's community License Key
until another administrator accepts the terms. You can also stop all registration and renewal at
any time by disabling automatic renewal (EULA §4.1).

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you may also complain
to your local data protection authority.

## 7. Security

We protect the data we receive with reasonable technical and organisational measures, including
encrypted transport to the license server. No method of transmission or storage is completely
secure.

## 8. Children

The Software is not intended for anyone under eighteen (18), and we do not knowingly receive their
personal data (EULA, _How you accept this Agreement_).

## 9. Changes

We may update this notice. The current version is always published at the same address, with its
date. If a change materially affects how we use personal data we already hold, we will tell the
administrators of registered instances by email before it applies.
