This Acceptable Use Policy ("**AUP**") forms part of the Dudamel End User License Agreement
(`EULA.md`). Capitalised terms have the meaning given there. It applies to you, your Authorized
Users, and every Agent you run on the Software. You are responsible for any breach of it through
your instance, whether by a person or by an Agent.

## 1. Prohibited uses

You must not use the Software, or let an Agent use it, to:

**Unlawful activity**
1. break any applicable law or regulation, or help anyone else do so;
2. infringe, misappropriate or violate intellectual property, trade secrets, publicity rights or
   other rights of any person.

**Unauthorised access and data misuse**
3. access, probe, scan, scrape, collect, copy, exfiltrate or disclose systems, accounts or data
   that you are not authorised to access, or exceed the access you were given;
4. process personal data without a valid legal basis, required notices and consents, or in
   breach of data-protection law; or combine data to identify, profile or track individuals
   without their knowledge where the law requires it;
5. process special categories of personal data or other sensitive data (such as health,
   biometric, genetic, financial-account, government-identifier or children's data) without the safeguards applicable law requires;
6. carry out surveillance or monitoring of individuals, including employees, that applicable law
   does not permit.

**Security**
7. create, distribute or run malware, ransomware, credential-harvesting, phishing or other
   malicious code or campaigns;
8. attack, disrupt or overload any system or network, or perform security testing on systems
   without the written authorisation of their owner;
9. circumvent the security, authentication, rate limits or usage policies of the Software or of
   any Third-Party Service.

**Deception and abuse**
10. defraud, deceive or mislead, including impersonating a person or organisation, or presenting
    AI-generated content or Agent actions as made by a human where the law requires disclosure;
11. send spam or unsolicited bulk messages, or messages that breach anti-spam or
    telecommunications law;
12. harass, threaten, defame or abuse anyone, or generate or distribute hateful, violent or
    sexually explicit content involving non-consenting adults;
13. generate, store or distribute child sexual abuse material, or any content that sexualises
    minors. Dudamel reports such material to the competent authorities when it becomes aware of
    it.

**High-risk uses**
14. make, without meaningful human review and the measures applicable law requires, automated
    decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on individuals, including on credit,
    insurance, employment, housing, education, healthcare or access to essential services;
15. operate critical infrastructure, weapons, medical devices, or any system where a failure
    could cause death, personal injury or severe environmental damage;
16. provide legal, financial, medical or other regulated professional advice to third parties
    without the qualified human oversight that applicable law requires.

**Third-Party Services**
17. use any Third-Party Service, including AI model providers, in breach of its terms or usage
    policies — you must comply with the usage policies of every AI provider you connect.

## 2. Agents

Because Agents act with the access you give them, you must:

1. grant each Agent only the credentials and permissions it needs for its task;
2. require human approval before Agents take irreversible or high-impact actions (such as
   deleting data, moving money, merging to production branches, or sending external
   communications at scale) unless you have assessed and accepted that risk;
3. monitor Agent activity and stop any Agent that behaves unexpectedly.

## 3. Enforcement

Dudamel has no visibility into your instance and no obligation to monitor its use. If Dudamel
becomes aware of a suspected breach of this AUP, it may ask you for information about it, which
you will provide in good faith. A breach is a material breach of the EULA and may lead to
suspension or termination of your licenses and non-renewal of your License Keys, in addition to
any other remedy. Dudamel may report suspected unlawful activity to the competent authorities and
cooperate with them as the law requires or permits.
